GPUPCB

GPUPCB is an interactive, live-updating 3D simulator of electromagnetic fields for PCB working completely in GPU, simulating transient states using finite-difference time-domain method (FDTD) and frequency-domain response finite-difference frequency-domain method (FDFD).

Users will be able to easily visualize and measure placement and intensity of sizes of physical fields such as electric, electric displacement, magnetic, magnetic displacement, current density, power dissipation, Poynting vector fields present on the board. That will allow users to inspect the impact of phenomena taking place, including skin effect and proximity effect, and look for causes of signal distortion and differential signal skew.

The tool will include built-in functions for measuring parameters of transmission lines (wave impedance, differential impedance, effective dielectric constant) and for electromagnetic compatibility (EMC) (crosstalk), high-speed signal integrity (SI) (skew, standing-wave ratio (SWR), S-parameters) and power integrity (PI) (power-supply rejection ratio (PSRR)).

GPUPCB will allow simulations of boards designed in KiCad or any other software with export to Hyperlynx format. Application will be available for all most popular desktop operating systems - Windows, Linux, MacOS and also a web-version will be available.

For a taste of how interactive, live-updated FDTD looks like, please take a look at the demo at https://cemsim.com/ GPUPCB will be showing signals live in a similar way.
